LFTools (https://github.com/lfhacks/LFTools)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Copyright 2026 Ian Mathews (awes145). GNU General Public License v3.
src/usb.rs ports its Leapster 2 mount/eject method (lf_dev.c: find the disk
whose SCSI product contains "2-SD", send it the vendor commands C2 to mount
and C6 to eject, keep track of whether it is mounted). What LeapLoader does
differently is listed at the top of src/usb.rs.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ribbit (the emulator)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The look and parts of the code (theme, fonts setup, frog icon drawing,
window and GPU setup, settings file, file dialogs, game tags) are ported
from Ribbit, used with the permission of its author, who owns both projects.
Each file says what came from where.

Fonts copied from the Ribbit emulator (crates/ribbit-frontend/src/fonts).
The frog icon is drawn at build time from Noto Emoji (SIL Open Font License 1.1),
which ships with egui (epaint_default_fonts).

Rust crates used by the app carry their own licences (MIT and/or Apache-2.0
for most); see each crate's metadata.
